# Downloaded Papers

## Must Read

1. ["It was 80% me, 20% AI": Seeking Authenticity in Co-Writing with Large Language Models](must_read/hwang_2024_authenticity_cowriting_llms.pdf)
   - Authors: Angel Hsing-Chi Hwang, Q. Vera Liao, Su Lin Blodgett, Alexandra Olteanu, Adam Trischler
   - Year: 2024 preprint / CSCW 2025 proceedings version
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.13032
   - Why relevant: Most directly addresses authenticity in AI-assisted writing and separates authenticity of process, self, and output.

2. [Using Large Language Models to Generate Authentic Multi-agent Knowledge Work Datasets](must_read/liu_2024_authentic_multiagent_knowledge_work.pdf)
   - Authors: Desiree Heim, Christian Jilek, Adrian Ulges, Andreas Dengel
   - Year: 2024
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.04286
   - Why relevant: Useful negative/control case for "authenticity" claims because it operationalizes authenticity as realism ratings over synthetic artifacts.

3. [Turning Large Language Models into Cognitive Models](must_read/binz_2023_turning_llms_into_cognitive_models.pdf)
   - Authors: Marcel Binz, Eric Schulz
   - Year: 2023
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03917
   - Why relevant: Strong evidence that LLMs can be adapted to reproduce human behavioral patterns, which supports the analogy side of the hypothesis.

## Should Read

4. [The Real, the Better: Aligning Large Language Models with Online Human Behaviors](should_read/real_2024_aligning_llms_online_human_behaviors.pdf)
   - Authors: Guanying Jiang, Lingyong Yan, Haibo Shi, Dawei Yin
   - Year: 2024
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.00578
   - Why relevant: Shows direct alignment of LLM outputs to observed online human behavior.

5. [On the Possibilities of AI-Generated Text Detection](should_read/gehrmann_2023_possibilities_ai_generated_text_detection.pdf)
   - Authors: Souradip Chakraborty, Amrit Singh Bedi, Sicheng Zhu, Bang An, Dinesh Manocha, Furong Huang
   - Year: 2023
   - URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04736
   - Why relevant: Gives theoretical and empirical constraints on human-vs-AI text discrimination, which matters for testing the hypothesis behaviorally.

6. [MAGE: Machine-generated Text Detection in the Wild](should_read/li_2024_mage_machine_generated_text_detection.pdf)
   - Authors: Yafu Li, Qintong Li, Leyang Cui, Wei Bi, Zhilin Wang, Longyue Wang, Linyi Yang, Shuming Shi, Yue Zhang
   - Year: 2024
   - URL: https://aclanthology.org/2024.acl-long.3/
   - Why relevant: Provides a practical benchmark and dataset for distinguishing human from model-authored text across domains.

## Reference

7. [Grief-Stricken in a Crowd: The Language of Bereavement and Distress in Social Media](reference/de_choudhury_2012_grief_stricken_in_a_crowd.pdf)
   - Authors: Jed R. Brubaker, Funda Kivran-Swaine, Lee Taber, Gillian R. Hayes
   - Year: 2012
   - URL: https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/view/14256
   - Why relevant: Grounds the death/bereavement side with a concrete linguistic study of distress expressions in human memorial writing.
